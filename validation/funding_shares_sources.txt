1. MEDICAID FMAP RATES (medicaid_adult, medicaid_child)
================================================================================

The Federal Medical Assistance Percentage (FMAP) determines the federal share
of Medicaid costs. Published annually in the Federal Register by HHS.

  UT FY2024: 65.90%  (state pays 34.10%)
  UT FY2025: 64.36%  (state pays 35.64%)
  TX FY2024: 60.15%  (state pays 39.85%)
  TX FY2025: 60.00%  (state pays 40.00%)

Sources:
  - Federal Register, FY2024 FMAP (effective Oct 1 2023):
    https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2022/12/05/2022-26390/federal-financial-participation-in-state-assistance-expenditures-federal-matching-shares-for
  - Federal Register, FY2025 FMAP (effective Oct 1 2024):
    https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2023/11/21/2023-25636/federal-financial-participation-in-state-assistance-expenditures-federal-matching-shares-for
  - KFF State Health Facts — FMAP by State:
    https://www.kff.org/medicaid/state-indicator/federal-matching-rate-and-multiplier/
  - MACPAC FMAP Exhibit:
    https://www.macpac.gov/publication/federal-medical-assistance-percentages-fmaps-and-enhanced-fmaps-e-fmaps-by-state-selected-periods/
  - Texas TMHP FY2025 FMAP notice:
    https://www.tmhp.com/news/2024-09-16-fmap-and-efmap-change-effective-october-1-2024

Note: medicaid_child uses the regular FMAP (not the enhanced E-FMAP which
applies to CHIP). Children covered under regular Medicaid are matched at
the standard FMAP rate.

2. CCDF MATCHING RATE (ccdf)
================================================================================

The Child Care Development Fund matching rate is based on the state FMAP.
Enhanced rates during the COVID-19 period have expired.

  UT FY2024: 65.90% federal  (standard rate = FMAP)
  UT FY2025: 64.36% federal
  TX FY2024: 60.15% federal
  TX FY2025: 60.00% federal

Previous value note: The code previously used 0.6840 for UT CCDF. This was
from the enhanced COVID phase-out period and has been corrected to the
standard rate (equal to FMAP).

Sources:
  - ACF FFY 2023 and FFY 2024 CCDF State Matching Rates:
    https://acf.gov/occ/data/ffy-2023-and-ffy-2024-ccdf-state-matching-rates

3. TANF — FEDERAL BLOCK GRANT AND STATE MOE (tanf)
================================================================================

TANF is a block grant. The federal grant has been fixed since 1996. States
must meet a Maintenance-of-Effort (MOE) requirement. The funding share is
computed as the ratio of federal grant to total (federal + state MOE).

  Utah:
    Federal block grant:  $75.4M
    State MOE:            $24.9M
    Federal share:        75.11%  (75.4 / 100.3)
    State share:          24.89%

  Texas:
    Federal block grant:  $484.65M
    State MOE:            $388.37M
    Federal share:        55.52%  (484.65 / 873.02)
    State share:          44.48%

Sources:
  - ACF TANF and MOE Spending and Transfers by Activity:
    https://acf.gov/ofa/data/tanf-and-moe-spending-and-transfers-activity-fy-2024
  - Utah Legislature Budget Office — TANF Overview:
    https://budget.utah.gov/what-in-the-tanf/
  - CRS TANF Primer (R48413):
    https://www.congress.gov/crs-product/R48413

4. SNAP (snap)
================================================================================

  All states: 100% federal / 0% state (for benefit payments)

SNAP benefits are funded entirely by the federal government. Administrative
costs are split 50/50 but are not modeled in the fiscal calculator.

Sources:
  - CRS SNAP Primer (R42505):
    https://www.congress.gov/crs-product/R42505

7. FEDERAL TAX CREDITS AND TAXES
================================================================================

  eitc_fed:        100% federal / 0% state   (federal EITC)
  ctc_fed:         100% federal / 0% state   (federal CTC)
  tax_income_fed:  100% federal / 0% state   (federal income tax)

These are federal programs by definition.

================================================================================
8. STATE TAX CREDITS AND TAXES
================================================================================

  eitc_state:        0% federal / 100% state  (state EITC)
  ctc_state:         0% federal / 100% state  (state CTC)
  tax_income_state:  0% federal / 100% state  (state income tax)

These are state programs by definition. For states without these programs
(e.g., Texas has no state income tax, state EITC, or state CTC), the PRD
computes $0 for these components, making the shares immaterial.

Sources:
  - Utah state EITC (20% of federal, non-refundable):
    https://incometax.utah.gov/credits/earned-income-tax-credit
  - Texas has no state income tax (TX Constitution Art. VIII, Sec. 24-a):
    https://taxfoundation.org/location/texas/
  - State EITC overview:
    https://www.taxcreditsforworkersandfamilies.org/state-earned-income-tax-credits/

NOTES
================================================================================

- FMAP rates are published by HHS in the Federal Register each November for
  the fiscal year starting the following October 1.
- TANF block grants have not changed since the program's creation in 1996.
  State MOE amounts should be verified annually from ACF data.
- For states not yet validated, UT 2024 defaults are used as placeholders.
- The CCDF matching rate equals the FMAP in the standard (non-enhanced) period.
